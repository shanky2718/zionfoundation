Zion FC Academy · School Football Programme Proposal
A programme of Zion Foundation · Ref. ZFC/SCH/2026/001
1. Executive summary
Zion FC Academy proposes to run a structured, year-round football programme on the school's 
campus for students aged 6 to 17. Students train 2–3 times a week in four age phases, follow a 
curriculum modelled on the FC Barcelona and Ajax youth methods, and receive a progress report 
every term.
The programme gives the school three things:
• A football programme for every interested student, from first touches at 6 to competitive 
football at 17.
• School teams coached and entered into inter-school tournaments.
• A real pathway: the best players can move up into Zion FC's club teams, Bangalore league 
football, and trials with Karnataka state and ISL academies.
Fees start at ₹1,500 a month, with no registration fee and a free welcome kit (section 6). That is 
below most venue-based Bengaluru academies, because training takes place on the school's own 
ground.
2. About Zion FC and our coaching philosophy
Zion FC is a Bengaluru football academy and club. The academy develops young players; the club 
gives them competitive teams to play for as they improve.
The programme is led on the pitch by Head Coach David Wale Oladunni, a former professional 
defender with more than 20 years in football across Africa and Asia (section 2.1), and Coach Rohan 
Kevin, an AFC C Licence coach with more than 10 years in youth development (section 2.2). [Add:

How it works in practice:

the school and parents can see.

10. Safeguarding, staffing and insurance
Zion FC will follow the school's child-protection policy in full, alongside our own.
• Coaches: every coach holds an AIFF or equivalent coaching licence (Head Coach David 
Wale Oladunni: AIFF D Licence; Coach Rohan Kevin: AFC C Licence, AIFF D Licence, AFC Futsal 
Licence and first-aid certified; [add any further coaches]), has current first-aid training, 
and has passed police verification before working with students.
• Staffing: a head coach responsible for the programme, plus an assistant coach per batch; 
a female coach or staff member present for girls' groups.
• Supervision: students are signed in and out; no child leaves before a parent or authorised 
person collects them. Coaches are never alone with a single child out of view.
• Health: parents complete a medical and emergency-contact form on joining. Training is 
adapted for heat, rain and air quality, with water breaks every 20 minutes.
• Injuries: first aid on site, parents informed immediately, and an incident report shared with 
the school within 24 hours.
• Insurance: Zion FC holds public liability cover for its sessions [confirm insurer and cover], 
and recommends personal accident cover for each student.
• Conduct: a code of conduct for players, parents and coaches, signed on registration. Zero 
tolerance for bullying or abusive language.
